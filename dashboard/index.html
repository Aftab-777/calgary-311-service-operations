SOURCE AND SCOPE

A dated public snapshot

City of Calgary 311 Service Requests, dataset iahh-g8bj. Each row is a request record.

Duplicate (Open) and Duplicate (Closed) records are shown separately and excluded from operational measures.

Addresses and exact coordinates are not included in this project.

MEASURE DEFINITIONS

Designed to be explained

Median closure time

The midpoint of valid calendar-day closure durations for nonduplicate Closed records. A same-day closure is zero days.

90th percentile

The duration at or below which approximately 90% of valid closed records fall, using linear interpolation. It is not an official service standard.

Open workload

Requests in this submission cohort whose status is Open when downloaded. A month filter selects when they were submitted, not their historical status that month.
